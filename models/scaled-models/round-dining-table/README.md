| Piece | From part | Print orientation | Print size (mm) | File |

- Chair1 (auto): `z <= (cleg_h) + (seat_t)`

## Joints

| Between | Bond | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Chair1 / Chair2 | glue | glue (56.25 mm² contact) |

Pegs are print millimetres and do not change with the scale: 2 mm diameter, 3 mm long, holes 0.3 mm wider and 0.5 mm deeper. Peg positions are set at build time, so rebuild after changing sizes. If the fit is too tight or loose, change `pegs.clearance` in `model.json`.

## Warnings

- No room for a peg between Chair1 and Chair2 (56.2 mm2 contact): glue that joint
- Table touches no other piece on a flat axis-aligned face
